## Why DEQ had only two, exactly

Not a two-grant research limit: Search performs no automatic deep research, uses an **80-detail shortlist**, and permits up to five cards. Only two inspected records passed screening. Five of its eight queries hit the 100-record cap; the complete federal opportunity universe was not retrieved. Queries and result counts are in `summary.json`.

- **336 of 416 DEQ records had no inspected official detail**. Their applicability is unknown, not proven irrelevant or ineligible. They are now labelled explicitly as unassessed because of the detail cap. No detail-fetch error occurred in the final runs.
- **144 DEQ records had a deadline/status hold**, and four had closing dates before the selected threshold. Counts overlap: forecasted, missing-date and expired records are not equivalent, and these are internal screening classifications, not invented official statuses.
- **6 DEQ records have confirmed direct-applicant restrictions; 7 have likely/interpretive restrictions**. Lack of a supported alternate route is kept distinct from a confirmed legal exclusion. **39 title-level affiliation/special-applicant holds** include CESU/tribal/fellowship routes not established by the project. These categories overlap and do not mean 52 distinct grants were legally ineligible.
- **67 inspected DEQ records lacked specific project-activity support**. Uninspected records are no longer assigned that negative finding. Title-labelled research/purpose holds can also occur without full details; they are screening interpretations, not completed NOFO reviews.
- **27 regional-title holds, 3 explicit non-NC eligible-location lists and 2 western-scope holds** require a supported NC project route. They remain auditable; name-based region/affiliation holds can create false negatives if a legitimate route exists outside the description.
- No funding-amount filter eliminated a DEQ candidate. The $300,000 request lies inside the two displayed official ranges; readiness, match and activity-specific limits remain unknown.
- The five-card display cap was **not binding** for DEQ. Retrieval caps, detail caps, unknowns and actual constraints—not a demand to manufacture five recommendations—explain the count.

See **[every plausible DEQ candidate and its exact disposition](deq-plausible-candidates.md)** (41 records: water/ecology titles, nonzero project evidence, and broad EDA infrastructure programs). **[All 416 retrieved DEQ IDs](deq-final.csv)** are included, not just the plausible cohort. It is impossible to enumerate all unreturned grants or declare uninspected ones unsuitable.

## Demonstrated defects and targeted fixes

1. Generic “restore”/“assessment” made NCDOT's road/drainage scope look ecological. Environmental groups now require water/ecology context, and road projects require actual road/culvert/transportation evidence. NCDOT's previous [363920 easements](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/363920) and [364169 RESTORE non-construction](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/364169) matches were removed; 363699 was already retrieved and had official details before the fix, but the old activity parser rejected it. Explicit road/culvert and flood-risk matching recovered it; this particular miss was screening, not retrieval.
2. Negated “rather than clinical trials” was a positive keyword, and generic mental-health fallback overlapped research/cancer grants. Negated activities are removed from positive fit/query terms, a mental-health topic is required, and research/clinical-study funding is held when the user proposes service delivery rather than research. Previous examples [357422](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/357422), [357421](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/357421), [357946](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/357946), [357947](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/357947), [356690](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/356690) are not direct service-delivery recommendations. These purpose holds are not applicant-ineligibility findings.
3. Missing official detail was labelled as ineligibility and insufficient relevance. Audit reasons now distinguish an uninspected detail cap, retrieval error, confirmed restriction, likely restriction and inspected lack of fit.
4. Broadband required a title hit when only one domain group matched. A sufficiently specific official broadband purpose can now support a program-brand title. [362843 Tribal Broadband](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/362843) remains held on applicant route; [362842 Native Entities](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/362842) is not established as NCDIT network-construction funding. [364030 Digital Newspapers](https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/364030) is not broadband deployment. ReConnect queries did not establish an open directly suitable NC opportunity; this is not a claim that the program never funds NC.
5. Official `responseDateDesc` was discarded, and no-date records could never pass even with documented rolling intake. The parser now preserves this source field; Search recognises explicit rolling intake only for a Posted record without a conflicting fixed date. It does not satisfy a future requested threshold. A maximum of two document-screening candidates, two document attempts each, can recover the demonstrated broadband permission from an official NOFO; no model is used and no uncertain route is marked verified.

Dashboard defaults and code, shared Sol citation/consistency checks, My Grants and the four Search inputs are unchanged. No source branch or original uncommitted file was edited.
